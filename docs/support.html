Common questions

How do I add a friend?

Go to the Friends tab, tap Add friend, and search for your friend by their handle. Send a request, and once they accept, you'll be connected.

How do I recommend something to a friend?

Open any film or show, choose to recommend it, pick the friend or friends you want to send it to, and add a note. It will arrive in their inbox.

Can I keep something private?

Yes. You can mark any title in your library as private, and it won't appear on your profile or to your friends.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Profile tab, open Account, and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all your data, and cannot be undone.
