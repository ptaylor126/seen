Delete your Seen account or data

Last updated: July 2026

Delete your account

Seen (by Paul Taylor) lets you delete your account and all associated data directly in the app:

  1. Open Seen and go to Profile
  2. Tap Account
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, library, ratings, reviews, recommendations sent and received, friends, photos, and feedback. This cannot be undone. Deletion is immediate; no additional data is retained.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual data in the app at any time: remove titles from your library, delete your ratings and reviews, delete your comments, and change or remove your profile photo. Deleted items are removed immediately.

If you can't access the app

Email thisispaultaylor@icloud.com with your Seen handle and we'll delete your account or data for you.
