# Privacy Policy for Seen

**Last updated: June 2026**

Seen ("we", "our", or "the app") is operated by Paul Taylor. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Seen app.

## Information We Collect

When you use Seen, we collect:

**Account information:** Your Apple ID (via Sign in with Apple), display name, handle, and avatar.

**Profile information:** Information you choose to add, including titles you've watched, are watching, want to watch, ratings, and notes.

**Social information:** Your friend connections, recommendations sent and received, and any private/public settings you choose for items.

**Device information:** Push notification tokens (if you opt into notifications), device type, and OS version for crash reporting purposes.

**Usage data:** Basic analytics about how you use the app (which features you use, frequency of use) to help us improve the product.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide the core features of the app (tracking titles, friend connections, recommendations)
- Send you notifications about friend activity (only with your permission)
- Improve the app based on usage patterns
- Communicate with you about important changes

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your information to third parties.

We share information only:
- With other users you've connected with as friends (visible profile information, titles you've added, ratings - unless marked private)
- With service providers who help us run the app (Supabase for database hosting, TMDB for film/TV metadata, Apple for authentication and push notifications)
- When required by law

## Third-Party Services

Seen uses the following third-party services:
- **Supabase** for authentication, database, and backend services
- **TMDB (The Movie Database)** for film and TV metadata
- **Apple** for Sign in with Apple authentication and push notifications

Each service has its own privacy policy that governs their handling of your data.

## Your Choices

You can:
- Mark individual items as private so friends cannot see them
- Remove friends at any time
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Disable push notifications in your device settings

## Account Deletion

To delete your account, go to Profile → Account → Delete Account. Your account enters a 30-day grace period during which you can restore it by signing back in. After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## Children's Privacy

Seen is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app.
